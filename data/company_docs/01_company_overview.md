# NovaWave Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Company Overview

**Document ID:** NW-GEN-001 | **Version:** 3.2 | **Effective:** 01 April 2026 | **Owner:** Corporate Communications

## 1. About Us
NovaWave Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a software services and product company founded in 2014 and headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. We build cloud platforms, web applications and AI-driven automation solutions for clients in banking, healthcare, retail and logistics.

- **Headquarters:** 4th Floor, Olympia Tech Park, Guindy, Chennai – 600032
- **Branch offices:** Bengaluru (Whitefield), Coimbatore (Tidel Park)
- **Employee strength:** Approximately 1,250 employees
- **CEO:** Ramesh Iyer
- **Head of HR (CHRO):** Lakshmi Narayanan
- **Website:** www.novawave-tech.example
- **General enquiries:** info@novawave-tech.example | +91 44 4000 1234

## 2. Vision and Mission
- **Vision:** To be the most trusted technology partner for businesses that want to grow with intelligent software.
- **Mission:** Deliver reliable, secure and human-centred technology while creating a workplace where people learn and grow.
