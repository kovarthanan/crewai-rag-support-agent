# Code of Conduct and Work Ethics

**Document ID:** NW-ETH-004 | **Version:** 2.3 | **Effective:** 01 April 2026 | **Owner:** Ethics & Compliance Office

## 1. Purpose
This Code sets out the standards of behaviour expected from every employee, contractor and intern at NovaWave Technologies. All employees must read and digitally acknowledge this Code on NovaHub **within 15 days of joining** and **every year in April**.

## 2. Professional Behaviour
- Treat colleagues, clients and vendors with respect and courtesy.
- Be punctual for meetings and available during core working hours (10 AM – 4 PM IST).
- Communicate respectfully on all channels, including email, Teams and social media.
- Do not use abusive language, threats or intimidation.

## 3. Dress Code
- **Monday to Thursday:** Business casual (collared shirts, trousers, formal footwear, or formal Indian wear).
- **Friday:** Smart casual (neat jeans and T-shirts allowed; no shorts or slippers).
- **Client visits:** Business formal.

## 4. Anti-Harassment and POSH
- NovaWave has **zero tolerance** for sexual harassment, as per the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act).
- Complaints can be raised with the **Internal Committee (IC)** at icc@novawave-tech.example within **3 months** of the incident.
- The IC completes its inquiry within **90 days** of receiving a complaint.
- All employees must complete POSH training annually.

## 5. Equal Opportunity and Anti-Discrimination
We do not discriminate on the basis of gender, religion, caste, age, disability, marital status, sexual orientation or region. Hiring, promotions and rewards are based only on merit.

## 6. Conflict of Interest
- Employees must disclose any situation where personal interests may conflict with the company's interests (e.g., a relative working with a vendor or competitor).
- **Moonlighting** (working for another employer, paid or unpaid, during employment) is **not permitted** without written approval from HR.
- Personal open-source contributions and teaching/blogging are allowed if they do not use company code, data or time, and do not compete with company business.

## 7. Gifts and Hospitality
- Employees may accept gifts from clients or vendors worth up to **₹2,000** per occasion.
- Gifts above ₹2,000 must be declared to the Ethics Office within **5 working days**.
- Cash, gift cards or vouchers must never be accepted, regardless of value.

## 8. Confidentiality and Data Protection
- Client and company information must be used only for business purposes.
- Never share confidential information on personal email, personal cloud storage or public AI tools.
- Comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 when handling personal data.

## 9. Use of Social Media
- Do not post confidential information, client names or project details on social media without approval from Corporate Communications.
- Clearly state that opinions are personal when discussing industry topics.

## 10. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption
Offering or accepting bribes, kickbacks or facilitation payments is strictly prohibited and will result in termination.

## 11. Workplace Safety and Substance Use
- Alcohol, drugs and smoking are prohibited inside office premises. Smoking is allowed only in designated zones.
- Report safety hazards to admin@novawave-tech.example immediately.

## 12. Whistle-blower Policy
- Employees can report suspected violations anonymously through the Ethics Hotline **1800-200-7788** or ethics@novawave-tech.example.
- NovaWave protects whistle-blowers from retaliation. Any retaliation is itself a serious violation.

## 13. Disciplinary Action
Violations are handled based on severity:
1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning / suspension
4. Termination of employment

Serious misconduct (fraud, harassment, data theft, violence) can lead to **immediate termination** without prior warnings.
