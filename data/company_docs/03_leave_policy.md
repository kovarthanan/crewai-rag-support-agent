# Leave and Holiday Policy

**Document ID:** NW-HR-003 | **Version:** 5.1 | **Effective:** 01 January 2026 | **Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility, how to apply, and the rules for carry-forward and encashment. The leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**.

## 3. Earned Leave (EL)
- Accrues at **1.5 days per month** of service.
- Can be used after completing the probation period (6 months); leave accrued during probation is credited.
- Must be applied at least **7 days in advance** for leave of 3 days or more.
- Maximum carry-forward to the next year is **30 days**; balance above 30 is lapsed on 31 December.
- Encashment of EL (up to 30 days, calculated on basic salary) is done only at the time of separation.

## 4. Casual Leave (CL)
- **8 days** per year, credited on 1 January (pro-rated for new joiners).
- Maximum **3 consecutive days** of CL at a time.
- CL cannot be combined with EL.
- Unused CL lapses at the end of the year.

## 5. Sick Leave (SL)
- **10 days** per year, credited on 1 January.
- A **medical certificate** is required for sick leave of more than **2 consecutive days**.
- Sick leave can be applied after the absence (within 2 working days of returning to work).
- Unused SL can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days.

## 6. Maternity Leave
- Female employees are eligible for **26 weeks** of paid maternity leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017.
- For the third child onwards: **12 weeks**.
- Adoption (child below 3 months) and commissioning mothers: **12 weeks**.
- Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.
- Eligibility: minimum **80 days** of service in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery.
- After maternity leave, employees may opt for up to **3 months of work-from-home** subject to role suitability.

## 7. Paternity Leave
- **10 working days** of paid paternity leave, to be used within **3 months** of the child's birth or adoption.

## 8. Bereavement Leave
- **5 working days** of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law).

## 9. Marriage Leave
- **5 working days**, available once during employment, to be used within 1 month of the wedding date.

## 10. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
- Granted only after all eligible leave balances are exhausted.
- Maximum LWP: **30 days** in a year, with approval from the reporting manager and HR.
- Unapproved absence for **3 or more consecutive days** is treated as unauthorised absence and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 11. Compensatory Off (Comp-off)
- Employees who work on a weekend or public holiday at the manager's request receive **1 comp-off** per full day worked.
- Comp-off must be used within **60 days**; otherwise it lapses.

## 12. How to Apply for Leave
1. Log in to **NovaHub → Leave → Apply Leave**.
2. Select leave type, dates and reason.
3. Leave is routed to the reporting manager for approval.
4. Managers must approve or reject within **2 working days**; otherwise it is auto-escalated to the skip-level manager.
5. For emergencies, inform the manager by phone/email and apply on NovaHub within 2 working days of returning.
