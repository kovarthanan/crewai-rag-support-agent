# IT Usage and Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** NW-IT-006 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Effective:** 01 April 2026 | **Owner:** IT & Information Security

## 1. Company Assets
- Every employee receives a company laptop on day one. Developers receive a laptop with **32 GB RAM**; other roles receive **16 GB RAM**.
- Laptops are refreshed every **4 years**.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT helpdesk (ext. 3000) **within 24 hours** and a police complaint (FIR) copy must be submitted.
- Damage due to negligence may be recovered from the employee as per the asset's depreciated value.

## 2. Passwords and Access
- Passwords must be at least **12 characters** with upper-case, lower-case, number and special character.
- Passwords expire every **90 days** and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** via Microsoft Authenticator is mandatory for email, VPN and NovaHub.
- Accounts are locked after **5 failed login attempts**; contact IT helpdesk to unlock.
- Never share passwords, OTPs or access cards with anyone, including IT staff.

## 3. Acceptable Use
- Company systems are for business use; limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work.
- Installing unapproved software is not allowed. Request software through the **IT Service Portal**; standard approvals take **2 working days**.
- USB storage devices are **blocked by default**. Exceptions require approval from the Information Security team.
- Use of public generative AI tools with client or confidential data is prohibited. Use the approved internal tool **NovaGPT** instead.

## 4. Email and Communication
- Official communication must happen only through company email and Microsoft Teams.
- Auto-forwarding company email to personal accounts is blocked.
- Report suspected phishing emails using the **"Report Phish"** button in Outlook.

## 5. Data Classification
| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Website content, press releases | No restriction |
| Internal | Policies, org charts | Share only within NovaWave |
| Confidential | Client contracts, source code, salary data | Need-to-know; encrypt when sharing externally |
| Restricted | Customer PII, credentials, financial data | Access approved by data owner; never store on local drives |

## 6. Security Incident Reporting
Report any security incident (malware, data leak, lost device, suspicious activity) immediately to **security@novawave-tech.example** or the IT helpdesk. Incidents must be reported within **1 hour** of discovery.

## 7. Mandatory Training
All employees must complete **Information Security Awareness training** within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Failure to complete training will result in temporary suspension of system access.

## 8. IT Helpdesk Service Levels
| Priority | Example | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 – Critical | Laptop not booting, account locked before client call | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 – High | VPN not working, email issues | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 – Medium | Software installation | 4 hours | 2 working days |
| P4 – Low | New accessory request | 1 working day | 5 working days |
