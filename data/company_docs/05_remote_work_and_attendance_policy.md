# Hybrid Work and Attendance Policy

**Document ID:** NW-HR-005 | **Version:** 1.4 | **Effective:** 01 June 2026 | **Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Hybrid Work Model
- NovaWave follows a **hybrid model: 3 days in office and 2 days work-from-home (WFH)** per week.
- **Tuesday and Thursday** are mandatory "Team Days" when all team members must be in office.
- Teams may choose the third office day with their manager's agreement.

## 2. Eligibility
- New joiners must work **from office full-time during the first 30 days** for onboarding.
- Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan may be required to work from office full-time.
- Client-facing roles follow the client's work-from-office requirements, which override this policy.

## 3. Full Remote Work Requests
- Temporary full remote work (up to **4 weeks**) may be approved for medical reasons or family emergencies by the reporting manager and HR.
- Permanent remote work is approved only in exceptional cases by the business unit head.
- Working from outside India is **not permitted** without prior written approval from HR and Legal due to tax and data-protection rules.

## 4. Attendance
- Attendance is captured via office access cards on office days and via NovaHub "WFH check-in" on home days.
- Employees must log a minimum of **40 hours per week**.
- Missed punch / attendance regularisation must be requested on NovaHub within **3 working days**. A maximum of **4 regularisations** is allowed per month.
- Late arrival after core hours start (10:00 AM) on more than **3 occasions** in a month will be flagged to the manager.

## 5. Home Office Requirements
- A stable internet connection of at least **50 Mbps** is expected for WFH.
- Employees receive an internet reimbursement of **₹1,000 per month**.
- A one-time **home office setup allowance of ₹5,000** is available (chair, monitor stand, headset) after confirmation.
- Company laptops must not be used on public Wi-Fi without the company VPN (NovaConnect).

## 6. Overtime and Shift Allowance
- Employees working in **night shifts (after 9:00 PM)** receive a shift allowance of **₹500 per night**.
- Cab drop facility is provided free of charge for women employees leaving the office after 8:00 PM and for all employees leaving after 10:00 PM.

## 7. Business Travel
- Travel must be pre-approved on NovaHub.
- Domestic travel: economy airfare / 3AC train; hotel limit of ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 in other cities.
- Daily allowance (per diem): ₹1,500 per day for domestic travel.
- Travel expense claims must be submitted within **15 days** of returning.
