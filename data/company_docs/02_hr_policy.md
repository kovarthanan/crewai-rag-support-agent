# HR Policy Manual

**Document ID:** NW-HR-002 | **Version:** 4.0 | **Effective:** 01 April 2026 | **Owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy explains how NovaWave Technologies hires, onboards, reviews, rewards and separates employees. It applies to all full-time, part-time and contract employees in India.

## 2. Recruitment
- All open positions are first posted internally on NovaHub for **7 working days** before being advertised externally.
- Employees who have completed **12 months** in their current role may apply for internal job postings (IJP) with their manager's knowledge.
- Interview process: HR screening → technical round(s) → managerial round → HR discussion.
- Relatives of current employees may be hired, but they cannot report directly or indirectly to each other.

### 2.1 Employee Referral Programme
| Level of hire | Referral bonus |
|---|---|
| Junior (0–3 years) | ₹15,000 |
| Mid-level (3–8 years) | ₹30,000 |
| Senior (8+ years) | ₹50,000 |

The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes **90 days** in the company. Referrals from HR and the hiring manager of that position are not eligible.

## 3. Onboarding and Probation
- New joiners must submit educational certificates, previous employment relieving letters, PAN, Aadhaar and bank details within **7 days** of joining.
- Induction programme: **3 days** covering company culture, policies, information security and POSH awareness.
- Every new employee is assigned a **buddy** for the first 60 days.
- **Probation period: 6 months.** It may be extended once by up to 3 months based on performance.
- Notice period during probation: **15 days** from either side.

## 4. Employment Confirmation and Notice Period
| Grade | Notice period after confirmation |
|---|---|
| G1–G3 (Associate to Senior Engineer) | 60 days |
| G4–G5 (Lead / Manager) | 90 days |
| G6 and above (Senior Manager, Director) | 90 days |

Notice period may be bought out or waived only with approval from the business unit head and HR. Unused earned leave cannot be adjusted against the notice period.

## 5. Performance Management
- The performance cycle runs from **1 April to 31 March**.
- Goals are set in April, a mid-year review happens in **October**, and the annual review is completed in **April**.
- Rating scale: 5 – Outstanding, 4 – Exceeds Expectations, 3 – Meets Expectations, 2 – Needs Improvement, 1 – Unsatisfactory.
- Employees rated 2 or below are placed on a **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** of 60 days.
- Annual salary revisions are effective from **1 July** each year.

## 6. Promotions
- Promotion cycles happen **twice a year: April and October**.
- Minimum time in current grade: **18 months**, with a rating of 4 or above in the most recent review.

## 7. Compensation and Payroll
- Salary is credited on the **last working day of every month**.
- Payslips are available on NovaHub by the **1st of the following month**.
- Payroll cut-off for attendance and reimbursements: **20th of each month**. Claims after that are processed in the next month.
- Annual variable pay (performance bonus) is paid in **June** based on company and individual performance.

## 8. Benefits
- **Group Health Insurance:** ₹5,00,000 family floater covering employee, spouse, up to 2 children and parents (or parents-in-law). Premium for employee, spouse and children is paid by the company; parental cover is optional and premium is deducted from salary.
- **Group Term Life Insurance:** 3 times annual CTC.
- **Group Personal Accident Cover:** ₹25,00,000.
- **Provident Fund (PF):** 12% of basic salary contributed by both employee and employer.
- **Gratuity:** As per the Payment of Gratuity Act, after 5 years of continuous service.
- **Learning allowance:** Up to ₹25,000 per financial year for approved certifications and courses.
- **Internet reimbursement:** ₹1,000 per month for employees on hybrid or remote work.
- **Meal card:** ₹2,200 per month (tax-saving).
- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** Free and confidential counselling for employees and families, 24x7.

## 9. Reimbursements
All reimbursement claims must be submitted on NovaHub with original bills within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 60 days will not be accepted.

## 10. Separation
- Resignations must be submitted through NovaHub.
- Exit interview is conducted by HR in the last week of service.
- Full and Final (F&F) settlement is processed within **45 days** of the last working day.
- Relieving letter and experience letter are issued along with the F&F settlement after all company assets are returned.

## 11. Grievance Redressal
Employees can raise grievances with their reporting manager, then the HR Business Partner. If unresolved within **15 working days**, the matter is escalated to the Grievance Committee chaired by the CHRO.
