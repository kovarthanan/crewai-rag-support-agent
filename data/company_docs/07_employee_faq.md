# Employee FAQ

**Document ID:** NW-GEN-007 | **Version:** 1.6 | **Updated:** 01 September 2026 | **Owner:** HR Shared Services

**Q: How many leaves do I get in a year?**
A: 18 Earned Leaves, 8 Casual Leaves and 10 Sick Leaves, plus 10 fixed holidays and 2 optional holidays.

**Q: Can I take Casual Leave and Earned Leave together?**
A: No. Casual Leave cannot be combined with Earned Leave.

**Q: When is salary credited?**
A: On the last working day of every month. Payslips are on NovaHub by the 1st of the next month.

**Q: How do I download my payslip or Form 16?**
A: Go to NovaHub → Payroll → Payslips / Tax Documents. Form 16 is published by **15 June** every year.

**Q: What is the notice period?**
A: 15 days during probation; 60 days for grades G1–G3 and 90 days for G4 and above after confirmation.

**Q: How do I add my parents to the health insurance?**
A: Parents or parents-in-law can be added during the annual enrolment window in **March** on NovaHub → Benefits. The premium for parental cover is deducted from salary.

**Q: How many days do I need to work from office?**
A: 3 days a week. Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory Team Days.

**Q: My account is locked. What should I do?**
A: Call the IT helpdesk at ext. 3000 (24x7) or email itsupport@novawave-tech.example.

**Q: Can I work from abroad for a few weeks?**
A: Not without prior written approval from HR and Legal.

**Q: Is moonlighting allowed?**
A: No, not without written approval from HR.

**Q: How much is the learning allowance?**
A: Up to ₹25,000 per financial year for approved courses and certifications. Apply via NovaHub → Learning → Reimbursement before enrolling.

**Q: When do appraisals and increments happen?**
A: Annual review is in April and salary revisions are effective from 1 July. Promotions happen in April and October.

**Q: Who do I contact for harassment complaints?**
A: The Internal Committee at icc@novawave-tech.example. You can also use the anonymous ethics hotline 1800-200-7788.

**Q: How long does Full and Final settlement take?**
A: Within 45 days of your last working day.

**Q: Is there a cab facility?**
A: Free cab drop is provided for women employees leaving after 8:00 PM and for all employees leaving after 10:00 PM.
